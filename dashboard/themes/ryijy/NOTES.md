# Ryijy

- **Idea:** the day is a knotted ryijy rug: 48 half-hour columns, woven up to now, bare warp after. A past day is a finished rug.
- **Moves with:** the daylight stretch (`--sunrise`/`--sunset`) is knotted in woad; the sun rides it. `notice` turns border, rings and hearts madder.
- **Shapes:** squares and steps only: stepped corners, knot-check dividers, running-stitch edges, fringe top and bottom.
- **Type:** Barlow Condensed (display) and Atkinson Hyperlegible (text). Palette tokens at the top of `theme.css`.
