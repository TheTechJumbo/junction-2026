# Talviryijy

- **Idea:** the whole page is one winter ryijy. Knotted fringe hangs off the top and bottom, a border band of snowflake stars and stepped diamonds runs across both ends, and check columns run down the sides. The picture field is the Kaamos courtyard knotted on a 160 × 160 knot grid: snowy tin roofs, chimneys, two spruces, the cathedral dome far off, the neighbours' windows and Anna's lamp-lit window. Below the roofline the field is the wall of her house, and the day is knotted into it as flat wool panels with stepped corners.
- **The sky is real:** `data-sky` recolours the six knotted sky bands (registered `@property` colours, so they fade): blue-hour indigo at night, with stars and the moon; a rose and gold band of dithered knots low over the roofs at low sun and twilight; pale cold blue by day. The sun is a knotted disc that follows `--sun-progress`/`--sun-altitude`, snapped to whole knots with `round()`. The page field follows too: wall-dark at night, snow-light by day.
- **Weaving:** the courtyard is always finished. Today is still on the loom: the day row in the sun card (48 half-hour knots) and the Rhythm runner are knotted up to now, the rest is bare warp, and an amber shuttle sits at the weaving edge. A past day is a finished rug.
- **Palette (from `assets/`):** night sky `#0b1029`, blue hour `#1d2a4a`, wall `#141c38`, slate `#3b4763`, snow `#eef2f6`, snow-shade `#c3cee0` (also the warp and fringe), Anna's lamp `#f2b04a` (the one warm accent: clock, now, primary actions), dusk rose `#d99a9e` and gold `#f0c27e`, spruce juniper `#2e4a36`, band indigo `#2c3d6b`, madder `#9c2f2a` (only for things to notice).
- **Mood:** calm is indigo, snow and juniper: juniper diamonds in the band, a juniper "Things to notice" field with a knotted heart. A day to notice swaps in `band-notice.svg` (madder diamonds), madder fringe knots, a madder knot-diamond before the headline and a madder field, and the lamp's dithered halo reaches a ring further and breathes faster.
- **Rhythm of the day:** Ryijy's runner: 24 hour-columns, one weft row per kind (door, pills, glasses, questions), with Kaamos's daylight stretch knotted from `--sunrise` to `--sunset` (rose, gold, pale, gold, rose). Alerts are madder knots with a snow ring.
- **Type:** Barlow Condensed 600/700 for display, sturdy like woven lettering; Albert Sans 400/600 for reading, open and warm. Both SIL OFL, self-hosted.
- **Motion:** a few loose snow knots drifting down, smoke knots from Anna's chimney, the lamp halo breathing, stars twinkling in steps, the shuttle moving slowly along its column; panels are knotted in on load (under 600 ms). Everything reads with motion off.
- Every SVG is drawn from knot charts by `source/gen.py` (run it from this folder). `band.svg` and `band-notice.svg` are the border-band tiles that theme.css repeats.
