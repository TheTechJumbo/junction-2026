# Talviryijy

- **Idea:** Kaamos knotted as a Ryijy rug: the snowy courtyard on a 160 × 160 knot grid, framed by fringe, a star-and-diamond border band and check columns.
- **Moves with:** `data-sky` recolours the six knotted sky bands; the sun is snapped to whole knots. Today is still on the loom up to now. `notice` swaps in `band-notice.svg` and madder.
- **Type:** Barlow Condensed (display) and Albert Sans (text). Palette tokens at the top of `theme.css`.
- Every SVG is drawn from knot charts by `source/gen.py` (run it from this folder).
