# Signal

- **Idea:** a calm bedside instrument, the whole day on one dial. A 24-hour face with midnight at the bottom and noon at the top; the sea-glass arc is today's daylight (`--sunrise` to `--sunset`, drawn with `stroke-dasharray`), and the amber hand turns with `--now`, its tip breathing slowly.
- **One indicator:** a status lamp under the hub. Off with no data, steady sea-glass when calm or quiet, one coral light (slow pulse) when the backend raised alerts. The "to notice" readout goes coral too; the rest of the display steps back.
- **Rhythm:** the same dial again, with four lanes (pill box, door, glasses, questions, from outside in) so marks a few minutes apart never collide. The words sit in a list beside the dial (under it on a phone).
- **Palette (from scene.svg):** graphite housing `#14181a` / `#1c2124`, bezel `#2c3437`, sea-glass phosphor `#7fe0c4` (dim ticks `#3d6e62`), printed legend `#8b9895`, amber hand `#f0a35b`, coral notice `#f08a74`. When `data-sky="day"` the instrument sits in daylight: pale housing `#e6e9e6`, ink-teal display `#16705d`, burnt amber `#c26a1e`.
- **Type:** IBM Plex Mono for numerals and the small-caps legend, like a printed instrument face, and IBM Plex Sans for reading. Both are SIL OFL, self-hosted (latin, 400/600 and 400/500, about 76 KB).
- **Shapes:** fine hairline rules, 4 px machined corners, and modules laid edge to edge like a faceplate, with no cards or shadows.
