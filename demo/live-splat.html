Live Gaussian splat

Turns what your camera sees into a 3D Gaussian-splat scan, live, as you look around.

  1. Each frame, a small AI model (Depth Anything V2) estimates how far away every pixel is.
  2. Pixels become coloured 3D Gaussians, placed using the phone's orientation sensors.
  3. When a spot is seen again, its Gaussian is merged: it gets more solid and tighter. Gaussians that turn out to be empty space (something moved) fade away.

Stand in one spot and pan slowly. The first start downloads a 20–30 MB model, which is then cached.
