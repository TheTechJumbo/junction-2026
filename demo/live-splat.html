Live Gaussian splat

Turns the real room into a 3D Gaussian-splat scan, live, as you move your phone.

AR scan (recommended on this phone)

Uses the phone's own AR tracking and depth (ARCore): real-time, true metres, and you can walk around. Splats appear on top of the real room as they are created.

AI depth scan

Works on any phone: a small AI model (Depth Anything V2) guesses depth from the camera image a few times a second. Stand in one spot and pan. First start downloads a 20–30 MB model.

In both modes, points within 2 cm merge into one Gaussian: seen again, it gets more solid and tighter; if the camera now sees past it (something moved), it fades.
