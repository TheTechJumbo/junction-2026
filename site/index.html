What it does

Help in the moment. Peace of mind at home.

The person wears the glasses and just asks. A few cheap sensors at home make sure the important things are noticed. The family gets a calm day, and an alert only when something is really off.

Did I take my pills?

Answered from when the pill box was really opened, with the time. When it is due, a reminder; the arrow shows the way to the box.

Where are my keys?

The glasses remember where things were last seen, and the display points there: around the furniture, in metres.

A quiet night, or not

The family app replays the night in 3D: up twice, 17 minutes out of bed, the front door at 03:11. Trip hazards on the way are marked.

Your glasses

Pick a pair. Watch it live.

Both apps list the glasses they can work with. Right now one pair is live: Seb's Ray-Ban Meta, worn at Junction 2026. Any other Ray-Ban Meta glasses join with a pairing code.

  1. Pair the glassesIn the Meta AI app, as usual. Daylight's glasses app connects through Meta's Device Access Toolkit.
  2. Share themThe home's vision relay prints a pairing code and link (live/share.py). The code is watch-only: it shows the display, never the camera.
  3. WatchIn the family app, tap Glasses, choose the pair and enter its code: you see what the glasses show, as it happens.

Receipts, not claims"The pill box was opened at 08:02", never "you took your pills". Dosing questions are refused.
